Prey size rule: a creature can only eat another smaller than this × its own size (0.5–1.0). Big-jump chance: how often a newborn gets one gene completely re-rolled. Mutation size: the biggest normal nudge, as a share of each gene's range. Escape chance: when on, prey escape an attack with chance strength × (prey speed ÷ hunter speed) + 0.2 × prey fear (between 5% and 95%), and a failed attack costs the hunter 2% of its max energy. Escape strength (0.3–1.0): higher means prey get away more often. Meat efficiency (0.1–1.0): how much of a meal's energy a hunter keeps.
